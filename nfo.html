Bio

Bienvenido Cruz is a New York-based director and designer from Canada. His career started at the age of fifteen and has spanned architecture, art, advertising, radio, and film — from Hong Kong to Stockholm. His early experimental film and web work drew notice: in 2003 The New York Times featured his collaboration with the electronic act Circlesquare, Sub-Reminisce, calling it "mesmerizing," and a festival review of his film-title design compared it to the work of Saul Bass. His work has appeared in Fast Company and International Designer's Network, and has been recognized with multiple Cannes Lions, One Show Cubes, and Art Directors Club Pencils, and a Platinum OBIE — the first and only in OUTFRONT's 86-year history.

For almost seven years, he led the practice of film and motion graphic design at AKQA's trailblazing New York studio, overseeing creative execution and building the production workflows and infrastructure that served clients including Verizon, Olympus, Maserati, Nike, and Calvin Klein.

As Associate Creative Director at XLabs — OUTFRONT Media's innovation group — his work spans content development, innovation, and the brand framework tools that scale it, from AI-driven production pipelines to brand expression and product development.

Offline, he is the founder of Analog Object Studio, a New York atelier that hand-binds archival notebooks for people who think better on paper.

On screens and on paper, the work keeps returning to one idea: the tools that made creative work easier also made it more uniform, and the things worth making are still worth making by hand. It's the same conviction behind this site — hand-coded, no framework, no template.
